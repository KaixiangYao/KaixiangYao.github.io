Kaixiang Yao

I am a Robotics Engineering undergraduate at Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), advised by Prof. Chenglong Fu. I am also a visiting undergraduate researcher at UC Berkeley, working with Prof. Masayoshi Tomizuka and Yuxin Chen.

My research interests are in robot learning and embodied AI for physical manipulation, especially contact-rich, deformable, and dexterous tasks. My previous work spans rigid-soft contact, physics-based digital twins, and assistive manipulation.
